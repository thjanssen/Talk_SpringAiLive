# Cancellation Policy

> **Demo content.** The Pirate's Chest is a fictional shop built for a Spring AI /
> Vaadin demo application. No real orders exist, so no cancellation or refund
> is ever actually processed. This page exists so the support assistant (and,
> later, a policy page in the UI) has realistic content to refer to.

## Right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from an order within **14 days** without
giving any reason. The withdrawal period starts on the day you (or a third
party named by you) receive the goods.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, contact the Quartermaster support chat
with your order number, or send an unambiguous statement (e.g. by email to
`support@pirates-chest.example`, a fictional address) stating your decision
to withdraw. Meeting the 14-day deadline is enough -- you do not need to
have returned the goods yet.

## Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from an order:

- Any payment received for that order (in this demo, none is actually
  collected) would be refunded within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal
  notice.
- Refunds would use the same payment method used for the original order,
  unless otherwise agreed.
- Goods must be returned within 14 days of notifying us of the withdrawal.
  You bear the direct cost of the return shipment.
- A reduction in value caused by handling the goods beyond what is
  necessary to check their nature and function may be deducted.

## Exceptions

The right of withdrawal does not apply to:

- Goods made to your specifications or clearly personalized (e.g. an
  engraved cutlass).
- Sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health or hygiene
  reasons once unsealed (e.g. an eye patch).
- Digital content (e.g. a downloadable treasure map) once downloading has
  started with your prior express consent.

## Order cancellation before shipment

While an order still shows as *placed* and has not yet shipped, you may ask
the Quartermaster support chat to cancel it directly -- no formal withdrawal
notice is needed in that case.

## Questions

Ask the Quartermaster in the support chat, or reach out to
`support@pirates-chest.example` (fictional address, for demo purposes only).
