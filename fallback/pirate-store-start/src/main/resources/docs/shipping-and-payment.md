# Shipping and Payment Terms

> **Demo content.** The Pirate's Chest is a fictional shop built for a Spring AI /
> Vaadin demo application. No real orders are shipped and no real payment is
> processed. This page exists so the support assistant (and, later, a policy
> page in the UI) has realistic content to refer to.

## Shipping

- **Delivery area:** We currently deliver to all seven seas -- in practice,
  every country selectable at checkout.
- **Delivery time:** Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days after an order
  is placed. Remote islands (and anywhere outside the EU) may take up to 10
  business days.
- **Shipping cost:**
  - Orders of 50.00 gold coins or more: free shipping.
  - Orders below 50.00 gold coins: a flat rate of 4.90 gold coins.
- **Order confirmation:** After checkout you receive an order confirmation
  with your order number on screen; no shipment tracking is provided in this
  demo.
- **Damaged or missing goods:** Contact the Quartermaster support chat with
  your order number and a description of the issue.

## Payment

- **Accepted payment methods (demo only):** Gold coins and pearls, weighed
  and counted at the harbor master's scale.
- **When payment is charged:** In this demo, no payment is actually collected
  or charged -- placing an order only simulates a completed purchase.
- **Currency:** All prices are shown in gold coins and include statutory VAT
  where applicable.
- **Invoices:** A simple order summary is shown on the confirmation page;
  no separate invoice document is generated in this demo.

## Questions

Ask the Quartermaster in the support chat, or reach out to
`support@pirates-chest.example` (fictional address, for demo purposes only).
